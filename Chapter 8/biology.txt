Temperature Rise: Human activities, primarily burning fossil fuels, lead to a steady increase in the Earth's average surface temperature.
Greenhouse Effect: Increased carbon emissions trap heat within the atmosphere, significantly intensifying the planet's natural greenhouse effect.
Environmental Impact: Rising temperatures cause polar ice caps to melt, leading to dangerous sea-level rises.
Climate Instability: Weather patterns are becoming increasingly erratic, resulting in more frequent and severe droughts, floods, and storms.
